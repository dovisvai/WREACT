WREACT

Delete your account and data

For the Android app com.wreact.app

You can delete everything WREACT holds about you. This page covers both routes — the direct one, and what to do if you have already uninstalled.

If you still have the app — do it yourself, instantly

  1. Open WREACT
  2. Go to the You tab
  3. Choose Delete Athlete Account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

This is immediate and permanent. No request, no waiting, no one reviewing it.

This removes:

  • Every reaction time you have posted, including from the public leaderboard
  • Your display name, chosen country, avatar, streak, run count and badges
  • The anonymous account itself

Your times stop counting toward your country's average as soon as they are gone. Nothing is retained, and nothing is recoverable afterwards — including by us.

If you have already uninstalled

Uninstalling the app does not delete data held on our servers. Email dovis.vai@gmail.com with the subject WREACT account deletion request.

One honest complication. WREACT accounts are anonymous by design — we hold no email address, phone number or real name, so there is nothing to match a request against automatically. To find your record we need you to tell us:

  • The display name you used
  • The country you competed for
  • Roughly when you played, and any reaction time you remember posting

We will locate the matching account and delete it. If the details are too thin to identify one record with confidence, we will say so rather than delete somebody else's.

Reinstalling does not recover an account. A fresh install creates a new anonymous account, so if you want your history gone, uninstalling and reinstalling is not the way — and if you want it kept, deleting is irreversible.

What is kept afterwards

Nothing that identifies you. Aggregate national averages are recalculated without your times rather than preserved with them. Server logs used for rate limiting are transient and are not a profile of you.
