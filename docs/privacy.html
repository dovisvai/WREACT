WREACT

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026 · applies to the Android app com.wreact.app

WREACT is a reaction-time game. You post times, they contribute to your country's average, and countries are ranked in a weekly table. This page describes exactly what the app collects to do that, and what it does not.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Reaction times — millisecond results, false starts, game mode, and when each run happened The product. Times form your personal record and your country's average.
An anonymous account ID, created automatically on first launch So your times belong to you and one device cannot post as another player.
Display name, avatar and country, all chosen by you How you appear on the leaderboard.
Streak and run counts, badges Your progress.
A push notification identifier, if you allow notifications To send match alerts. Refusing notifications means this is never created.
Purchase receipts, if you subscribe To verify the subscription and unlock features.
IP address, at our server Rate limiting, to stop automated abuse of the leaderboard.

2. What we do not collect

  • No location. The app requests no location permission and never uses GPS. Your country is the one you pick — the app suggests one from your device's time zone, and you can choose any other.
  • No email address, phone number or real name. There is no sign-up. The account is anonymous.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no ad tracking.

3. What is public

The leaderboard is public. Your display name, chosen country and reaction times are visible to anyone using the app. Choose a display name you are comfortable being seen. Nothing else in the list above is shown to other players.

4. Who else receives data

ServiceReceives
Google FirebaseThe anonymous account ID, your profile and your times. Stores and authenticates them.
OneSignalOnly if you allow notifications: the push identifier and the gameplay values used to decide which alerts are relevant — your country, your national rank, how many more athletes your country needs, your streak, your best reaction time, the current matchday, and whether you have a subscription. If you refuse notifications, none of this is sent.
RevenueCatThe anonymous account ID and your Google Play purchase receipt, to validate a subscription. No card or payment details ever reach us or them.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the three services above, which exist to make the app work.

5. How long it is kept

Times and profile data are kept until you delete them. Deletion is immediate and permanent — see below. Rate-limiting records at our server are transient and are not retained as a profile of you.

6. Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete Athlete Account. This removes your scores, your profile and the anonymous account itself. It cannot be undone.

If you have already uninstalled and cannot reach that button, use the account deletion request page.

7. Children

WREACT is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. As no email or real name is collected, we hold nothing that identifies a person by name in any case.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the in-app copy is updated to match.
